# 20260707 cVEP NBRS/JFPM task structure note

## Current role

This task is the local reproduction task for Zheng et al. 2024 cVEP/NBRS/JFPM data.
It should stay focused on baseline classification and paper-aligned figures for:

- `FBCCA-CODE`
- `TRCA`
- `MSTRCA`

The released data are raw EEG after 250 Hz down-sampling. The task runner performs
the classification preprocessing locally: channel selection, 50 Hz notch,
Chebyshev-I filter bank, and latency-aware stimulus-window crop.

## Fixes validated on 2026-07-06

Two issues were verified and fixed:

1. Notch filter alignment:
   - Earlier cVEP runs used a 50 Hz comb notch.
   - The paper states a 50 Hz second-order IIR notch.
   - The task now uses `scipy.signal.iirnotch(50, Q=35)` plus `filtfilt`.

2. MSTRCA scoring alignment:
   - Earlier `MSTRCA` estimated multi-stimulus neighborhood filters but used
     non-ensemble target-specific scoring.
   - The corrected implementation uses ensemble TRCA-style scoring by default.
   - This moved `MSTRCA` close to the paper:
     - `NBRS-15`: 4 s accuracy `83.73%` vs paper `83.85%`; best ITR `87.22` vs `87.71`.
     - `NBRS-8`: 4 s accuracy `89.56%` vs paper `90.27%`; best ITR `104.88` vs `105.09`.
     - `JFPM-8`: 4 s accuracy `87.07%` vs paper `87.65%`; best ITR `91.59` vs `95.12`.
