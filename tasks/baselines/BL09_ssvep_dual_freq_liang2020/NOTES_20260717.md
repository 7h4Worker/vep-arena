# NOTES 2026-07-17

## Scope

Prepared a formal Liang2020 classification runner after reviewing the supplied
paper PDF. The goal is to run the full downloaded dataset once with the paper's
main recognition family, not just the previous inventory smoke.

## Paper Facts Used

- EEG: 9 channels `Pz, PO5, PO3, POz, PO4, PO6, O1, Oz, O2`, sampled at 1000 Hz.
- Preprocessing: remove the first 140 ms visual latency; band-pass/filter-bank
  with zero-phase forward/reverse IIR filtering.
- Recognition: individual training-data TRCA templates; filter-bank weighted
  decision.
- ITR: data window plus 0.5 s gaze-switching time.
- Experiment 3: offline 40-target comparison, 4 blocks per condition, 80 trials
  per block. The runner uses leave-one-block-out CV.
- Experiment 4: online-style 40-target comparison, paper split is first 6 blocks
  for training and last 3 blocks for testing. The runner uses runs 1-6 train and
  7-9 test by default; local run 10 files, where present, are ignored in the
  paper split.

## Condition Mapping

- `exp1` condition 1: Method 1 fast gradient descent.
- `exp1` condition 2: Method 2 fast optimization.
- `exp1` condition 3: Method 3 global search.
- `exp2` condition 1: Method 1 fast gradient descent.
- `exp2` conditions 2-4: Method 4 random codes, worst/median/best.
- `exp3` condition 1: longest frequency-distance zero-phase dual-frequency.
- `exp3` condition 2: Method 1 frequency-pair selection with zero phase.
- `exp3` condition 3: Method 1 dual-frequency and phase encoding.
- `exp4` condition 1: Method 1 dual-frequency and phase encoding.
- `exp4` condition 2: single-frequency phase JFPM comparison.

## Implementation Decision

The formal default includes TRCA and eTRCA only. TDCA/FBDCCA require exact
target-level frequency/phase references. The complete references are embedded in
paper figures and appendix panels, not distributed as a machine-readable
codebook in the local dataset. Do not mix unvalidated OCR-derived codebooks into
the main reproduction table.
