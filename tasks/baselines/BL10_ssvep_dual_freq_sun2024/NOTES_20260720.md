## Figure 2 Phase Codebook Completion

The public `code of 40Targets.xlsx` file contains the trigger number and
left/right-eye frequencies, but it omits the initial phases used by the
optimized stimulus. Sun et al. (2024), Figure 2(III), reports both phases for
all 40 spatial targets. Those values were digitized in row-major target order
and added to `vep_arena.data.dual_frequency` as
`SUN2024_FORTY_TARGET_PHASES_PI`.

The completed codebook was validated against all 20 swap pairs: each swapped
target exchanges the left/right frequency and its associated phase. This
completion does not establish the undocumented author comb-filter transfer
function; it only makes phase-aware diagnostic filters possible.

## Comb Candidate Sample Comparison

Added `run_comb_candidates.py` to compare several plausible comb definitions
without changing the fixed protocol:

- subjects: S11/S13 development, S12/S14 confirmation;
- five target repetitions with target-repetition leave-one-out;
- all 64 EEG channels;
- fixed trigger-to-EEG offset: 0.14 s;
- windows: 0.6 and 1.0 s;
- standard baseline: continuous-data 8-90 and 16-90 Hz two-band TRCA.

The diagnostic candidates were:

1. `global_soft_comb_trca`: a global Gaussian-weighted union of the two
   fundamentals, their second harmonics, and their sum frequencies.
2. `pair_soft_comb_trca`: the same smooth response applied separately for
   each of the 20 swapped target pairs.
3. `pair_phase_aligned_comb_trca`: the pair response additionally rotates the
   complex spectral components using the Figure 2 initial phases.
4. `pair_periodic_comb_trca`: a fractional-delay-style periodic response with
   peaks at integer multiples of either fundamental.

Soft-comb width was tied to the observation window as `sigma = 0.5 / T` Hz.
Epoch filters used reflection padding and a zero-phase FFT implementation.
These are Arena sensitivity definitions, not claimed author implementations.

## Interpretation

None of the candidate comb definitions produced a stable improvement. The
periodic comb exceeded baseline only for S11 at 0.6 s (25.5% versus 24.5%) and
fell below it for every other subject/window combination. The global soft comb
retained more information than the pair-specific variants, but still remained
below the broad two-band baseline.

The phase-aligned result is especially informative: applying the display's
initial phases directly as an EEG spectral rotation was harmful. Neural phase
contains subject-, channel-, latency-, and harmonic-specific transfer effects;
it cannot be assumed equal to the stimulus phase. In addition, swap partners
share the same frequency-phase components and differ only in eye assignment,
so a scalar spectral comb cannot recover the key binocular distinction.

Swap-partner errors were only 4.6-8.9% of all errors across these methods. The
primary failure is therefore not merely choosing the wrong member of an
otherwise correctly identified pair. These negative results reinforce that
the missing performance mechanism is the paper's binocular spatial filter
(bTRCA/ebTRCA), not a target-frequency comb alone. No full-cohort comb run is
authorized from this smoke.

## Frequency-Response Audit

`plot_comb_responses.py` renders the actual diagnostic transfer functions for
the two-band baseline, global soft response, hard/soft/phase-aligned pair
responses, and periodic response. It shows both a widely separated pair
(targets 1/6) and a close pair (targets 2/36).

The response curves expose why the candidate definitions underperformed:

- the dense global codebook turns the global soft comb into an almost
  continuous 8-32 Hz passband with irregular shallow notches;
- close-pair fundamental, second-harmonic, and sum-frequency lobes merge;
- direct complex averaging of opposite stimulus phases causes destructive
  attenuation in the phase-aligned close-pair response;
- the periodic response passes many repeated peaks unrelated to the five
  empirically selected target components.

The phase-aligned and periodic definitions are therefore retained only as
negative ablations. They are not reasonable author-comb candidates after this
response audit.
