# 2026-07-20 Arena-Native TDCA Smoke

## Scope

Added a diagnostic Arena-native TDCA extension without changing the completed
official ETRCA/FBDCCA baseline outputs.

- subjects: S1-S3, fixed before the run rather than selected by performance;
- paradigms: Checkerboard Arrangement, Binocular Vision, Binocular-Swap Vision;
- channels: the same occipital 9 channels for every paradigm;
- windows: 0.4, 0.6, and 1.0 s;
- cross-validation: five-block leave-one-block-out;
- methods: TDCA and ETRCA on the same five TRCA filter-bank subbands;
- TDCA: five harmonics, `n_delay=5`, `n_components=8`;
- references: two frequencies per target with zero phase, because the public
  `Stimulate_Code.txt` does not provide phase values.

This is an Arena method extension, not an author TDCA reproduction.

## Reference Structure

Checkerboard Arrangement and Binocular Vision use the same 40 dual-frequency
codes, with 40 distinct unordered frequency-pair reference subspaces.
Binocular-Swap Vision has 40 ordered targets but only 20 unordered reference
subspaces: every target has a partner that swaps the two eye frequencies.

Arena TDCA can still distinguish the two members because it trains a separate
EEG class template for every target. The sinusoidal projection is identical
within a swap pair, so any within-pair separation comes from training EEG rather
than from the reference subspace.

## Subject-By-Paradigm Interaction

The sample does not support a single global good-subject/bad-subject ordering.

- S1 is weak for Checkerboard TDCA (16.0%, 27.5%, 53.5%) but strong for
  Binocular Vision (92.5%, 93.0%, 95.5%) and Binocular-Swap Vision
  (89.0%, 92.5%, 98.5%).
- S3 shows the opposite contrast: strong Checkerboard TDCA
  (84.5%, 93.0%, 97.5%), weaker Binocular Vision
  (41.5%, 51.5%, 61.0%), and intermediate Binocular-Swap Vision.
- S2 is comparatively stable and TDCA improves markedly over ETRCA for both
  binocular paradigms.

This crossover is a paradigm-by-subject interaction, not merely a generic EEG
quality ranking. It is the main reason a future full run should retain
subject-level curves rather than reporting only a cohort mean.

## Swap Errors

At 0.6 s, TDCA made 85 total Binocular-Swap errors across S1-S3; 13 were the
corresponding swap partner (15.3%). ETRCA made 119 errors; 19 were swap-partner
errors (16.0%). The duplicate TDCA reference subspace therefore does not cause
classification to collapse into systematic swap confusion. Most remaining
errors are between different frequency pairs.

## Decision

The native TDCA path passes smoke and is worth a full 35-subject window-curve
run. Full execution should preserve the controlled occipital-9 comparison and
may add a separate official-channel analysis for Binocular-Swap Vision; those
two channel scopes must not be pooled.

## Full 35-Subject Run

The controlled occipital-9 full run completed on 2026-07-20:

```text
tasks/ssvep_dual_alpha_baselines/results/tdca_full_occipital9_20260720
```

- subjects: 1-35;
- paradigms: CA, BV, and BsV;
- windows: 0.2-2.0 s in 0.2 s steps;
- methods: TDCA and same-condition ETRCA;
- workers: 14 with BLAS threads capped at one;
- completion: 105/105 subject-paradigm units;
- rows: 2,100 subject summaries and 420,000 trial predictions;
- main successful run time: about 18.3 minutes;
- manifest state: `complete`.

The first full launch exposed that every source epoch is exactly 500 samples.
TDCA training normally requests `window_samples + n_delay`, so 2.0 s requested
505 samples and failed before classification. The corrected runner loads the
available 500 samples and zero-pads only the five delayed tail samples at 2.0 s,
matching the existing TDCA test-side delay-padding rule. All 105 TDCA 2.0 s
summary rows record `tail_padding_samples=5`; every other row records zero.
The initial failed unit-manifest entries are retained as an audit trail and are
superseded by later complete entries.

At 0.6 s, full-cohort TDCA versus ETRCA was:

| Paradigm | TDCA | ETRCA | Delta | Subject wins/ties/losses |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Checkerboard Arrangement | 78.93% | 81.17% | -2.24 pp | 8 / 5 / 22 |
| Binocular Vision | 78.84% | 75.07% | +3.77 pp | 26 / 4 / 5 |
| Binocular-Swap Vision | 69.76% | 65.89% | +3.87 pp | 28 / 2 / 5 |

The first-three-subject smoke was optimistic for BsV (85.83% TDCA at 0.6 s)
relative to the full cohort (69.76%). It correctly predicted the direction of
the TDCA advantage but not the cohort accuracy level. Full-cohort results must
replace smoke means in later cross-dataset comparisons.

TDCA 0.6 s subject accuracies have near-zero cross-paradigm Spearman
correlations: CA-BV -0.095, CA-BsV -0.223, and BV-BsV -0.076. This supports a
strong paradigm-by-subject interaction and argues against treating any single
paradigm as a generic subject-quality screen.

At 0.6 s, BsV TDCA made 2,117 errors, of which 219 were the corresponding swap
partner (10.3%). ETRCA made 2,388 errors, with 220 swap-partner errors (9.2%).
The duplicated sinusoidal reference subspaces do not create systematic pair
collapse; most errors are between different frequency pairs.
