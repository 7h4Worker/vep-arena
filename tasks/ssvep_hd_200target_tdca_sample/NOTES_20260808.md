## Key findings for HD200

1. **Channel loss scales with target count** (r=0.91-0.99)
   - TDCA decoder benefits from spatial diversity
   - Unlike Benchmark (which benefits from channel reduction)

2. **HD200 is the "fastest channel" measured**: τ = 0.047s (vs Benchmark 0.351s)
   - 99.6% utilization (η) at 40 targets
   - Peak ITR = 469 bits/min (40 targets) / 593 bits/min (200 targets)

3. **Entered cross-paradigm envelope** as HD200_40t and HD200_200t entries
   - Both show fastest convergence of all paradigms tested
