## TDCA Codebook Smoke

The paper's Method 1 values were digitized from Figure 1(a): 40 two-frequency
codes selected from 8.2-16.2 Hz with a shared phase of 0, 0.5, 1, or 1.5 pi.
The figure's row-major spatial order did not match the public MAT target-label
order: only one of 40 entries matched under the direct order assumption and the
expected frequency pair had lower mean amplitude than unselected candidates.

`run_tdca_smoke.py` therefore performs an explicit one-to-one spectral alignment
between the digitized Figure 1 codes and Exp3-condition3 Subject 1 MAT labels.
The resulting mapped pair amplitude was 2.87 times the unselected candidate
amplitude. Applying that fixed map to Subject 2, without a new alignment, kept a
1.78 ratio and yielded Exp3 TDCA accuracy of 95.31% at 0.4 s and 96.88% at 0.6 s.

This is a diagnostic TDCA smoke, not an official paper result: the MAT-label
mapping is inferred from Subject 1 spectral data and still requires an
independent source or author confirmation before a full formal TDCA rerun.
The prior `smoke_tdca_method1_20260720` identity-order output is invalid for
codebook validation and is not a result to cite.

## Diagnostic Full TDCA Scope

Added `run_tdca_full.py` for the all-subject diagnostic run with the S1-derived
map frozen before the remaining subjects are evaluated. Its scope is limited to
the conditions that use the Figure 1 Method 1 codebook: Exp3-condition3 and
Exp4-condition1. It preserves subject-level summaries, predictions, unit status,
aggregate summaries, window curves, and 0.6 s aggregate confusion matrices.

The primary aggregate curve excludes S1 because S1 Exp3-condition3 data were
used to infer the MAT-label mapping. A future author-confirmed codebook/order is
still required before this task can be promoted from `diagnostic_full` to an
official result. Exp3-condition1 and condition2 also remain out of scope until
their distinct Figure 16 codebooks are digitized and independently checked.

## Full-Run Exp4 Trial-Count Check

The completed diagnostic full output has 45,800 prediction rows, rather than a
fixed 45,600-row count. This was traced to the supplied Exp4 S2 files: test
runs 7 and 8 contain 80 trials each and run 9 is absent, whereas the remaining
11 subjects have 40 trials in each of runs 7-9. The runner retains all available
paper-split trials and aggregates subject accuracies with an unweighted subject
mean, so this source-data difference does not give S2 extra aggregate weight.

The paper states the intended protocol as runs 1-6 for training and the final
three blocks for testing. It also reports S2's online accuracy at 96.25%, a
value consistent with a 160-trial denominator. The file-level deviation is
therefore retained and recorded rather than silently truncating S2 to 120 trials.
